QUESTIONS

Before you ask us to find it.

A few quick answers about how VELORA works.

What exactly can VELORA source? +

VELORA covers Food, Fashion, Agriculture, Home, Lifestyle, Business Supplies and Local Products. You can also send a request outside these categories.

Do I need to know the supplier? +

No. You tell us what you need and we explore suitable suppliers, producers, makers, retailers and other possible sources.

Can I request something not listed? +

Absolutely. The seven categories are designed to cover common needs, but they are not limitations. You can submit a custom request.

Can businesses submit requests? +

Yes. Businesses can use VELORA for product sourcing, bulk requirements, office supplies, packaging, retail needs and other business-related sourcing requests.

How does source checking work? +

We review the available information about a source or product to help identify an option that matches the request.
